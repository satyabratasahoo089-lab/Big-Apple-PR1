What kind of Web3 teams do you work with?ViewHide

We assess opportunities based on the product, its stage, the team’s objectives, and whether coordinated strategy, communications, creator, community, or ecosystem support is a good fit.

Do you only provide PR or creator campaigns?ViewHide

No. PR and creator work can be focused engagements, but our strongest work connects narrative, distribution, community, and activation around one objective.

Can you guarantee media coverage or user growth?ViewHide

No. We agree realistic objectives, deliverables, and measurement methods without guaranteeing editorial coverage, token performance, or specific user numbers.

Can we work together for one campaign?ViewHide

Yes, subject to fit, scope, timeline, and capacity. A focused campaign or broader growth partnership can begin with the same discovery conversation.

How is pricing determined?ViewHide

Fees depend on the objective, scope, channels, duration, required resources, and any external creator, media, or production costs.
